# Features Comparison

Comprehensive comparison of features and capabilities across our Basic, Pro Plus, and Enterprise plans to help you choose the right plan for your needs.

## Overview

Our pricing plans are designed to meet the needs of different users, from individual job seekers to enterprise teams. Each plan includes core features with additional capabilities as you upgrade.

## Plan Comparison

### Basic Plan

**Perfect for individual job seekers getting started**

```typescript
interface BasicPlan {
  price: {
    monthly: number;
    yearly: number;
    savings: number;
  };

    core: string[];
    limitations: string[];
    included: boolean;
  };
  limits: {
    resumeUploads: number;
    jobApplications: number;
    aiAnalysis: number;
    storage: string;
  };

```

**Included Features**:
- ✅ **Resume Analysis** (5 per month)
- ✅ **Job Matching** (basic algorithm)
- ✅ **Profile Management** (basic)
- ✅ **Job Search** (standard filters)
- ✅ **Application Tracking** (manual)
- ✅ **Email Notifications** (basic)
- ✅ **Mobile Access** (responsive web)
- ✅ **Basic Analytics** (simple metrics)

**Limitations**:
- ❌ **Advanced AI Features** (limited)
- ❌ **Priority Support** (community only)
- ❌ **Custom Integrations** (not available)
- ❌ **Team Management** (not available)
- ❌ **Advanced Analytics** (not available)
