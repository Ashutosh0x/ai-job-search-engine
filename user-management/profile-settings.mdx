# Profile Settings

Comprehensive profile management system to customize your account settings, personal information, and privacy preferences.

## Overview

Our profile settings system allows you to manage all aspects of your account, from basic information to advanced privacy controls and notification preferences.

## Key Features

### Account Information

- **Personal details** management and updates
- **Contact information** verification and editing
- **Profile picture** upload and management
- **Account credentials** and security settings
- **Email preferences** and notification settings

### Privacy Controls

- **Profile visibility** settings and controls
- **Data sharing** preferences and permissions
- **Contact information** privacy options
- **Search engine** indexing controls
- **Third-party** integration permissions

### Customization Options

- **Theme preferences** (light/dark mode)
- **Language settings** and localization
- **Notification preferences** and schedules
- **Display options** and layout customization
- **Accessibility** settings and accommodations

## Best Practices

### Profile Optimization

1. **Complete your profile** - Fill all required fields for better visibility
2. **Use professional photos** - High-quality, professional headshots
3. **Keep information current** - Regular updates ensure accuracy
4. **Optimize for search** - Use relevant keywords in your bio and skills

### Privacy Management

1. **Review settings regularly** - Check privacy settings monthly
2. **Limit data sharing** - Only share what's necessary
3. **Monitor activity** - Regularly review login history
4. **Use strong passwords** - Enable 2FA for enhanced security

### Security Best Practices

1. **Enable 2FA** - Use two-factor authentication
2. **Monitor logins** - Check for suspicious activity
3. **Update regularly** - Keep information current
4. **Use unique passwords** - Don't reuse passwords across accounts
