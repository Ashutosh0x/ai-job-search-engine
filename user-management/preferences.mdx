# Preferences

Control how JobSpark AI behaves for your account.

## App
- Theme: light, dark, or system
- Language & localization
- Accessibility (reduced motion, high contrast)

## Notifications
- Email alerts (job matches, application updates)
- In-app notifications
- Quiet hours

## Content
- Preferred industries and roles
- Salary expectations and currency
- Remote/hybrid preferences
