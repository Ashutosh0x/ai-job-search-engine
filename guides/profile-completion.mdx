# Profile Completion

Complete profiles rank higher and match better.

## Checklist
- Profile photo
- Headline and summary
- Experience and education
- Skills and certifications
- Location and preferences

## Tips
- Use clear, quantified achievements
- Keep information current
- Enable profile visibility according to your comfort
