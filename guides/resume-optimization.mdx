# Resume Optimization Guide

## Overview

Our resume optimization guide provides proven strategies, best practices, and AI-powered insights to help you create resumes that pass through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) while also engaging human recruiters.

## Best Practices

### Content Guidelines

1. **Use specific metrics** - Quantify achievements with numbers
2. **Focus on impact** - Highlight results, not just responsibilities
3. **Tailor to job** - Customize for each position
4. **Keep it concise** - One page for most roles, two for senior positions
5. **Use active voice** - Start bullet points with action verbs

### Format Guidelines

1. **Choose compatible fonts** - Arial, Calibri, or Times New Roman
2. **Use standard sections** - Experience, Education, Skills
3. **Avoid graphics** - No images, charts, or complex formatting
4. **Use simple bullets** - Standard bullet points, not symbols
5. **Include keywords** - Naturally integrate job-specific terms

### ATS Guidelines

1. **Use standard file formats** - PDF or DOCX preferred
2. **Avoid tables and columns** - Use simple text layout
3. **Include contact information** - Name, email, phone, location
4. **Use clear section headers** - Experience, Education, Skills
5. **Optimize for scanning** - Easy-to-parse content structure
