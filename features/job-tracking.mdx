# Job Tracking

Comprehensive job application tracking system to manage your job search progress and stay organized throughout the hiring process.

## Overview

Our job tracking system helps you monitor your applications, track interview progress, and maintain detailed records of your job search activities. Stay organized and never miss a follow-up.

- **Centralized tracking** of all job applications

## Best Practices

### Application Strategy

1. **Set realistic goals** - Track application targets and timelines
2. **Follow up systematically** - Use automated reminders for consistent follow-up
3. **Document everything** - Keep detailed notes for each application
4. **Analyze patterns** - Use analytics to improve your approach

### Interview Preparation

1. **Research thoroughly** - Company culture, recent news, interview process
2. **Practice regularly** - Use mock interviews and preparation tools
3. **Track feedback** - Document interview feedback for improvement
4. **Prepare questions** - Always have thoughtful questions ready

### Follow-up Strategy

1. **Timing matters** - Follow up within 1-2 weeks of application
2. **Be professional** - Keep communications courteous and concise
3. **Show enthusiasm** - Demonstrate continued interest in the role
4. **Accept outcomes** - Gracefully handle rejections and move forward
