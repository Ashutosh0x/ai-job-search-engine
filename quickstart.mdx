# Quick Start Guide

Welcome to JobSpark AI! This guide will help you get started with our AI-powered job search platform in just a few minutes.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- A valid email address
- Your resume in PDF or DOCX format (optional)

## Step 1: Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit JobSpark AI">
    Navigate to [jobspark.ai](https://jobspark.ai) in your browser
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Click the "Sign Up" button and enter your email address and password
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email">
    Check your email and click the verification link to activate your account
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Complete Your Profile

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Basic Information">

  <Card title="Profile Settings">

## Step 3: Upload Your Resume

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Resume Section">
    Go to the "Resume" section in your dashboard
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload File">
    Click "Upload Resume" and select your PDF or DOCX file
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get AI Analysis">
    Wait for our AI to analyze your resume and provide feedback
  </Step>
</Steps>
