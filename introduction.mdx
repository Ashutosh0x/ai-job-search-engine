# Welcome to JobSpark AI

JobSpark AI is a comprehensive, AI-powered job search platform that revolutionizes how job seekers find opportunities and how recruiters discover talent. Built with cutting-edge technology, our platform combines artificial intelligence, machine learning, and intuitive design to create the most efficient job search experience.

## What is JobSpark AI?

JobSpark AI is a full-stack web application built with **Next.js 14**, **React 18**, and **TypeScript**, featuring:

- 🤖 **AI-Powered Resume Analysis** - Get instant feedback and optimization suggestions
- 🎯 **Smart Job Matching** - AI algorithms match you with the perfect opportunities
- 📊 **Advanced Analytics Dashboard** - Track your job search progress with detailed insights
- 🔒 **Secure Authentication** - Built with Supabase for enterprise-grade security
- 🌍 **Multi-language Support** - Localized experience for global users
- 📱 **Responsive Design** - Works seamlessly across all devices
